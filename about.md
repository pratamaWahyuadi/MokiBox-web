## 🎯 Overview

**MokiBox** is a short-form video application built around the metaphor of a **"box of surprises."** Every scroll is like opening a new box—you never know what you'll find inside. It could be a laugh, a new perspective, or a moment that makes your day.

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| 🎥 Short-form video | Vertical, swipeable video feed |
| 📦 "Box" discovery | Curated content grouped into thematic "boxes" |
| 🎨 Creative tools | Filters, stickers, and effects with a playful twist |
| 👥 Community-driven | Share your "boxes" and discover others' |

## 🧠 Why "MokiBox"?

**"MokiBox"** emerged as a fresh, brandable name. Here's why:

### ✅ Strengths

| Aspect | Evaluation |
|--------|------------|
| **Unique & Memorable** | "Moki" is catchy and abstract; "Box" is universally understood. Easy to say and spell. |
| **Metaphor-Rich** | "Box" implies a container of surprises, treasures, or stories—perfect for a discovery-based platform. |
| **Visually Inspiring** | The "box" concept opens up endless design possibilities (unboxing animations, box-shaped UI elements, etc.). |
| **No Direct TikTok Conflict** | Does not use "-tiktok" suffix, significantly reducing trademark risk. |
| **Room for Growth** | Abstract enough to evolve beyond short videos into other "box" experiences. |

### ⚠️ Considerations

- **Existing Product**: A portable WiFi speaker called **MOKIBOX** was launched on Kickstarter around 2014 by MOKISOUND. However, the trademark that blocked it in one jurisdiction has been **cancelled** for non-use.
- **Not Descriptive**: The name doesn't explicitly say "video" or "social"—branding and tagline will need to bridge that gap.

## 🎨 Brand Identity

### Positioning

> **"A Box of Surprises, Every Scroll"**

In a world of predictable feeds, MokiBox is the place where every swipe feels like opening a gift. It's discovery, delight, and connection—wrapped in a box.

### Tagline

> **"Short Video"**

Short, punchy, and perfectly aligned with the "box" metaphor. It invites users to explore and reveal what's inside.

### Visual Identity

| Element | Direction |
|---------|-----------|
| **Logo** | A stylized box that "opens" or "unfolds" with a playful reveal animation. Consider a cute mascot peeking out of the box. |
| **Color Palette** | Warm, inviting colors—think kraft paper brown, vibrant orange, and cheerful yellow. Accents of teal or mint for contrast. |
| **Typography** | Friendly, rounded fonts with a slightly "playful" feel. The "Box" in the logo could be more geometric to suggest structure. |

### Tone of Voice

- **Playful & Curious:** Encourage exploration and surprise.
- **Warm & Inviting:** Make users feel like they're opening a gift from a friend.
- **Consistent:** Apply this tone across all touchpoints—notifications, social posts, in-app copy, and marketing.

## 🚀 Go-to-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: Pre-Launch (Build Anticipation)

| Activity | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Teaser Campaign** | Mysterious "closed box" visuals across social media with the question: *"What's inside?"* |
| **Waitlist Landing Page** | Countdown timer with "Unlock Your Box" CTA |
| **Influencer Teasers** | Send physical "MokiBoxes" to micro-influencers—actual boxes with surprise items and a QR code to the waitlist |

### Phase 2: Launch (Acquire Early Users)

| Activity | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **"Unboxing" Challenge** | Launch with a challenge where users share their own "unboxing" moments—could be literal or metaphorical |
| **Network Effects First** | Target one specific community or campus to create a sense of belonging |
| **Referral Rewards** | Reward early users for inviting friends to "open their box" |

### Phase 3: Post-Launch (Growth & Retention)

| Activity | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Daily "Box" Drops** | Curated collections of content released daily—like opening a new box every day |
| **Gamification** | Collectible "box stamps" or badges for daily engagement |
| **Creator Partnerships** | Onboard creators with exclusive "box" content series |

## ⚖️ Trademark Considerations

**Before launching**, conduct a formal trademark search:

| Jurisdiction | Authority | Class |
|--------------|-----------|-------|
| 🇮🇩 Indonesia | **DJKI** (DGIP) | Class 9 (Software) & Class 42 (Tech Services) |
| 🇺🇸 United States | **USPTO** | Class 9 & 42 |
| 🇪🇺 European Union | **EUIPO** | Class 9 & 42 |

**Existing Marks to Be Aware Of:**

| Mark | Status | Risk Level |
|------|--------|------------|
| **MOKIBOX** (speaker) | Trademark cancelled for non-use; product from 2014 | 🟡 Low-Moderate |
| **MemoBox** | Korean memo app | 🟢 Low (different category) |
| **MooBox** | iOS novelty app | 🟢 Low (different category) |

|------|---------|

| **kokitok** | ⚠️ **Considered** – Unique but required heavy branding lift |
| **MokiBox** | ✅ **Selected** – Fresh, metaphor-rich, and brandable |
